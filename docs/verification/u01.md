## Matrix conventions and coverage inspection

IDs are durable leaf identifiers, not runtime record IDs. `source_locator` uses
repository-relative `file:L<line>` against the fingerprinted input; source_text
is verbatim clause content with bullet/number prefixes removed. Context retains
heading and governing list parent for actual list members so a field such as
`owner` or `version` has a specific subject. A list parent stops governing when
the list ends. Global R3 prose uses Required States, not Approval State. Every
substantive requirement line, including prose, list
parents, enum members, literal names and tree paths, is covered. Headings,
separators, blank lines and code fences are formatting, not independent clauses.
R0 covers scenario, objective, exclusions and required tree; R1–R13 match numbered
sections, with R4.1/R4.2/R4.3 distinct.

Interview leaves are individual stakeholder semantic sentences and declared
source routes, with original time in context and exact line in locator. Repeated
assertions share a canonical meaning; existing repeated-route leaf IDs are retained.
Interviewer speech is outside the stakeholder inventory. Conversational prompts,
greetings and thanks are explicitly classified conversational/non-normative with
exclusion reasons. INT-080 and INT-146 remain in the matrix with their stable IDs,
no business acceptance fields, and status not-applicable-with-basis. Pain/pricing
narrative is context; its inclusion makes no procurement requirement. The stakeholder's authorization
to owners to update their own systems does not authorize source writes by this
tool. The interview's notice/role/authority descriptions are facts and candidate
interpretations to verify, never a substitute for captured applicable law.

Each row carries intended unit/component, implementation acceptance owner, case
IDs, individual assertion, evidence categories, status and future evidence
location where applicable; conversational rows carry exclusion reasons instead.
Cases include explicit stimulus, expected records and independent
artifact observations. Structural coverage is a baseline result; all runtime leaves
remain **not-demonstrated** with **not-yet-produced** evidence. No green case-level
result may close untested subassertions later. Documentary decision aliases B-REQ,
B-INT, B-SCHEMA, B-PLAN and B-README are not fabricated live evidence IDs.

The interview inventory is a fixed source-based oracle, not regenerated from the
matrix by the checker. It partitions all stakeholder text into 220 exact character
spans: 121 distinct normative meanings/declarations, two contextual meanings,
40 repeated assertions/source references and 57 conversational segments.
Offsets are zero-based character positions within the fingerprinted source line,
with exclusive end. Every non-whitespace character must be accounted for; all
duplicate and conversational exclusions have a reason. Each business meaning
maps to an exact source/leaf identity, and every retained interview matrix row
must appear in the inventory. A paragraph's one mapped sentence cannot cover
its other meanings. If both a meaning and its leaf are removed, source-span
coverage still detects the omission.

Manual inventory review by the Codex implementation agent compared the fixed
entries to the original stakeholder text, including feedback conditions at 03:37,
all three evidence labels at 04:41, all ten declared routes, notice/provenance
distinctions, role-specific decisions and repeated route references. Exclusions
were reviewed as conversational presentation or exact repeated meaning, not
missing business requirements. This is an agent inspection record, not stakeholder
approval. Automated checks prove source/inventory/matrix consistency; they cannot
prove that a classification or legal interpretation is semantically correct.
